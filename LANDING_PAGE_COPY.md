## Section 5 — How It Works (Desire)

**Step 1 — Search**
You apply any combination of 14 filters — job title, location, company size, tech stack, and more — and WarpLeads returns a precise list of matching contacts in real time.

**Step 2 — Reveal**
You click Reveal on any contact and instantly get their verified work email, personal email, and phone number for 1 credit. Contacts you've already revealed are always free to access again.

**Step 3 — Export or sync**
You export your leads to CSV, push them directly to your CRM, or save them to a named list inside WarpLeads — ready for your next campaign or sequence.

## Section 6 — Objection-Busting FAQ (Desire)

**How accurate is the contact data?**
Every email is validated for syntax, mail server records, and deliverability before you spend a credit. If an email comes back undeliverable, we refund the credit automatically.

**Do my free credits roll over if I don't use them?**
They don't — free credits reset to 20 every day at midnight UTC. The upside: you get a fresh 20 every single day with no action required.

**I already revealed a contact. Will I be charged if I export them again?**
No. Once revealed, a contact's details are stored in your account permanently. You can re-export, copy, or sync that contact to your CRM as many times as you want at no cost.

**Can my whole team use the same account?**
Yes. Team plans let you invite members, assign daily credit quotas per person, and share Reveal History across the team — so no one pays twice for the same contact.
